# Orbit

A fast, native game launcher for Windows, for **Roblox** and **Deadlock**. No webview, no Electron.

## Get it

Download **orbit.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/0xSnww/Orbit-Client/releases/latest) and run it. No installer needed.

Orbit updates itself. Updates are signed, and you can roll back to the previous version from Settings → About.

## Roblox

- **Pick your server.** See every server's location and ping, filter by region, and let **Smart join** pick the best one for you, even from the Play button on roblox.com.
- **Wait in line.** Join a full server and Orbit gets you in the moment a slot opens.
- **Manage Roblox.** Installs and updates Roblox, runs several windows at once, and handles mods, custom fonts and FastFlags. Import your setup from Bloxstrap or Fishstrap.
- **Per-game settings.** Give each game its own account, server rules, FastFlags, mods and outfit.
- **Accounts and avatar.** Sign in with a QR code, switch accounts in one click, and edit outfits without opening the website.
- **Friends and chat.** Live status, one-click join, alerts when friends start playing, and chat with friends who use Orbit.
- **In-game overlay.** An optional HUD with ping, server city and more, plus a hotkey menu (Ctrl+Shift+F9) to rejoin, hop servers or take a screenshot.
- **Private servers, badges and play time** on every game's page.

## Deadlock

- **Mods from GameBanana.** Browse, 1-click install, auto-update, and see what an install changes first.
- **Heroes and skins.** Pick a skin per hero and preview its 3D model.
- **Fix problems fast.** Play unmodded once, or let a guided test find the broken mod.
- **Stats and builds.** Your rank and recent matches, plus community hero builds.
- **Game settings.** Launch options, key binds and patch notes in one place.

## Also

- Discord Rich Presence, and optional alerts sent to a Discord channel.
- Themes, layouts and a Hub of community themes and crosshairs. Press Ctrl+K to jump anywhere.
- Sync your look and settings across PCs through OneDrive, Dropbox or Google Drive. Accounts and chat never leave your PC.

## Good to know

- Orbit never injects into or modifies the game client.
- Found a bug? Settings → About → **Save diagnostics** makes a zip to attach. Accounts, cookies and tickets are removed.

Orbit is closed source. This repo only hosts releases. Not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or Valve.
